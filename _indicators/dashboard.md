## Multi-Indicator Trading Dashboard

A powerful dashboard that brings data from all 6 Crypto4light indicators into one convenient table, allowing you to monitor multiple indicator readings across different timeframes without overcrowding your chart.

**Customize Your Dashboard**

Choose a color scheme that matches your chart theme and independently select the timeframe for each indicator. This gives you full flexibility to combine different timeframes and monitor the market exactly the way you trade.

**Save Chart Space. Get More Information.**

Instead of switching between multiple charts or displaying numerous indicators on your screen, you can quickly review all essential readings in one place. Identify current market conditions, compare signals across timeframes, and get a clearer overview of the asset you're analyzing.

**Built for Every Trading Style**

Whether you trade on 4H, analyze daily trends, or scalp on minute charts, the dashboard helps you combine multiple timeframes and indicator readings into your own trading strategy.

The table is designed for excellent readability on both desktop and mobile devices.

**Important:** This is not a standalone indicator and does not generate independent trading signals. It is an additional dashboard designed exclusively to display data from the 6 Crypto4light indicators.
