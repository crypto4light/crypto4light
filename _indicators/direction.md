## Crypto4light Direction – Multi-Timeframe Trend Analysis

Crypto4light Direction is a technical analysis indicator designed to help traders assess the prevailing market trend and identify potential changes in price direction across different timeframes.

It analyzes market behavior to highlight potential positioning activity by large market participants and identify areas where the current trend may be approaching its final stages.

The higher the selected timeframe, the broader the market perspective and the more useful the analysis can be for evaluating the prevailing direction of the market.

## Trend Direction & Trading Signals

The indicator generates two types of visual signals, each serving a different purpose.

### 1. Round Dots – Potential Positioning Activity

Depending on the current trend direction, round dots appear above or below price.

These signals highlight potential areas where large market participants, including market makers, may be gradually building or reducing their positions.

Large participants require sufficient liquidity to enter or exit substantial positions. As a result, accumulation and distribution can develop over an extended period rather than happening all at once.

**Important:** Round dots are not primary trading signals. They are designed to provide additional insight into potential positioning activity and changes in market behavior.

### 2. Triangles – Potential Trend Reversal

A red or green triangle represents a major trend-change signal.

Depending on the selected timeframe, it can indicate a potential reversal in either the local or broader market trend.

These signals suggest that significant positioning activity may have reached a stage where the balance between buyers and sellers is changing, potentially creating conditions for a new directional move.

* **Green Triangle** – Potential bullish trend reversal.
* **Red Triangle** – Potential bearish trend reversal.

Unlike the round dots, triangles are designed to highlight more significant changes in market direction and can be used as primary signals when evaluating potential trading opportunities.

## Trend Levels & Key Price Zones

Crypto4light Direction includes four important reference lines that help identify potential trend transition areas.

**Solid Green & Red Lines – Potential Trend End Levels**

These levels represent areas where the current trend is more likely to reach its final stages or experience a significant change in direction.

**Dashed Green & Red Lines – Potential Positioning Zones**

These levels highlight areas where large market participants may begin gradually accumulating or distributing positions before a broader change in market momentum develops.

Together, these levels provide additional context for evaluating the current trend and identifying potential turning points.

## Customization

All four reference levels can be adjusted through the indicator settings.

You can modify their positions according to your preferences or the characteristics of a specific asset.

The default settings are already configured to provide a balanced starting point, so additional adjustments are optional.

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Crypto4light Direction can be used across different timeframes depending on your trading style.

* **Higher Timeframes:** Weekly, Daily, and other larger intervals help evaluate the broader market direction and identify potentially significant trend changes.
* **Lower Timeframes:** Useful for monitoring local trend movements and analyzing potential entry and exit opportunities.

For a more comprehensive analysis, I recommend comparing signals and reference levels across multiple timeframes rather than relying exclusively on a single chart interval.

## Alerts & Notifications

The indicator supports the following TradingView alerts:

* **Bullish** – Potential bullish trend change.
* **Bearish** – Potential bearish trend change.
* **Possible Oversold** – Potential oversold market condition.
* **Possible Overbought** – Potential overbought market condition.

These alerts allow you to monitor important market developments without constantly watching the chart.

## Important Notes

Crypto4light Direction is designed to help traders evaluate market direction, monitor potential positioning activity, and identify possible trend transitions.

Its signals represent algorithmically identified market conditions and should not be interpreted as verified information about institutional or market-maker transactions.

No signal guarantees an immediate reversal or future price movement. Always combine the indicator with price action, broader market analysis, and proper risk management.
