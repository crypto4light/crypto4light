## Custom Automatic Fibonacci Grid for Post-Listing Tokens

A specialized indicator designed to automatically identify key trading zones for newly listed cryptocurrencies.

Based on my proprietary approach, the model uses just **one fully closed daily candle** to calculate important price levels and identify potential buying and distribution (selling) zones.

What previously required hours of manual chart analysis can now be done automatically in seconds.

For more precise entry planning, I recommend using the token's lowest listing price as an additional reference when analyzing potential buying opportunities.

### Zones & Visuals

The indicator automatically displays three key areas:

* **2 Buying Zones** – potential areas of interest for accumulation and entries.
* **1 Distribution Zone** – an upper selling area designed for swing trading opportunities.

You can customize line colors and visual settings to match your preferred chart layout.

### Model Invalidation

Every model has a defined invalidation level.

If price breaks below this level, the current Fibonacci model becomes invalid and should no longer be considered an active trading setup.

### Supported Timeframes

To ensure correct calculations, the indicator is designed for intraday and daily analysis.

* Supported: 1D / 12H / 4H / 1H
* Not supported: Timeframes above 1D and second-based charts.

### Important

This indicator is a technical analysis tool, not a magic formula or a guarantee of profitable trades.

Always combine its signals with proper risk management and broader market analysis.

### My Recommendation

Explore how this model performed on previously listed tokens. Studying historical post-listing price action can help you better understand how the identified zones behave under different market conditions.
