## Crypto4light Market Mood – Multi-Timeframe Market Sentiment

Crypto4light Market Mood is an indicator designed to visualize crowd sentiment and market interest in a particular asset.

It helps traders understand the prevailing psychological state of market participants and identify different stages of market sentiment, from optimism and euphoria to disbelief and depression.

The indicator allows you to analyze market sentiment across multiple timeframes. Higher timeframes generally provide a broader perspective of crowd psychology, while lower timeframes help monitor shorter-term changes in market sentiment.

## Market Sentiment Zones

The indicator uses a color-coded system to represent different stages of crowd psychology.

Each color corresponds to a specific sentiment condition:

* **Dark Red – Euphoria:** Extreme optimism and excitement, when market participants may become excessively confident about further price appreciation.
* **Light Red – Thrill:** Strong positive sentiment and growing excitement as the market continues moving upward.
* **Dark Orange – Strong Belief:** Market participants maintain strong confidence in the asset and expect the trend to continue.
* **Light Orange – Belief:** Traders remain confident in the market despite potential uncertainty or short-term corrections.
* **Yellow – Optimism:** Positive expectations begin to develop, with growing interest in the asset.
* **Green – Hope:** Early signs of improving sentiment, often appearing when market participants begin expecting a recovery.
* **Light Blue – Disbelief:** Market participants remain skeptical about the current price movement or potential recovery.
* **Dark Blue – Capitulation:** Strong negative sentiment, when many participants lose confidence and may exit their positions.
* **White – Depression:** Extremely weak market sentiment and a general lack of interest in the asset.

## Understanding Market Sentiment

Market sentiment does not always move in the same direction as price.

For example, during a downtrend, the indicator may still display an orange zone, representing Belief or Strong Belief.

This can mean that market participants remain confident that the price will recover or that the decline is only temporary.

Similarly, positive sentiment does not necessarily mean that price must continue rising, and negative sentiment does not automatically indicate that the market has reached its bottom.

The purpose of Crypto4light Market Mood is to help you understand what market participants may be feeling at different stages of the market cycle.

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

You can use the indicator on different timeframes to compare short-term and long-term sentiment.

* **Higher Timeframes:** Help identify broader crowd psychology and prevailing market sentiment.
* **Lower Timeframes:** Help monitor shorter-term changes in market interest and emotional behavior.

Comparing multiple timeframes can provide additional context when evaluating the current market environment.

## Customization & Alerts

The latest version includes renamed sentiment zones, making it easier to understand the indicator's readings and configure alerts.

You can enable notifications for the sentiment zones you want to monitor.

Some sentiment categories have two separate alerts because the indicator uses different intensity levels based on its input data.

For example:

* Belief
* Strong Belief

This allows you to choose the sentiment intensity that is most relevant to your trading strategy.

You can configure alerts for individual color zones and receive notifications when market sentiment enters the selected condition.

## Important Notes

Market sentiment is not a direct prediction of future price movements.

Crowd psychology can remain positive during a downtrend or negative during an uptrend for extended periods. Always consider the broader market structure, price action, and selected timeframe when interpreting the readings.

Crypto4light Market Mood is an analytical tool designed to help you understand market psychology and identify changes in crowd sentiment.
