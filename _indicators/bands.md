## Crypto4light Bands – Fair Price, Support & Resistance

Crypto4light Bands is a technical analysis indicator designed to identify key price levels and potential reaction zones based on its proprietary calculations and algorithm.

Its purpose is to help traders understand where price may react, identify potential market extremes, and analyze price behavior across multiple timeframes.

The indicator allows you to synchronize important levels from higher timeframes with lower-timeframe price action, providing a broader perspective of market structure and potential trading opportunities.

## Fair Price & Key Levels

The middle line represents the **Fair Price** – an estimated reference value calculated for the selected timeframe.

This value changes depending on the timeframe, allowing you to evaluate how current price compares with the calculated fair price across different market perspectives.

The indicator includes several key levels:

* **Inner Support & Resistance** – identifies the primary support and resistance areas around Fair Price.
* **Outer Support & Resistance** – highlights wider price boundaries and potential extreme zones.
* **Mid Extreme Lines** – additional reference levels positioned within each range to provide more detailed price analysis.

These levels are designed to help identify potential reaction areas, evaluate market conditions, and plan trades according to your preferred timeframe.

## Accumulation & Distribution Analysis

As a market trend develops, Crypto4light Bands can help identify potential accumulation and distribution phases.

During distribution, large market participants may gradually reduce their exposure or take profits as price approaches important upper ranges.

Similarly, during accumulation, price may approach lower ranges where buying interest could begin to increase.

Institutional participants typically build or reduce positions over time rather than executing everything at once. Monitoring price reactions around these levels can therefore provide useful context when evaluating broader market behavior.

The indicator can also generate **Buy and Sell signals**. These signals can be analyzed across different timeframes, including higher timeframes such as the Weekly chart.

Historical examples can help you evaluate how price reacted after confirmed signals and how the levels performed under different market conditions.

## Buy & Sell Signals

Crypto4light Bands includes automated Buy and Sell signals based on its internal calculations.

You can use these signals alongside Fair Price, support and resistance levels, and the broader market trend to evaluate potential trading opportunities.

The signals are intended to complement your analysis rather than replace independent confirmation and risk management.

## Customization

Every line and visual element can be customized through the indicator settings.

Available options include:

* Customize individual line colors and visual appearance.
* Enable or disable colored fills for extreme zones.
* Display only Buy/Sell signals.
* Adjust the visibility of individual levels and zones.
* Configure the chart appearance according to your preferences.

This flexibility allows you to create anything from a complete analytical layout to a minimal chart displaying only the levels and signals you need.

## Information Panel

By default, an information table is displayed in the upper-right corner of the chart. It can be disabled at any time.

The panel provides several useful metrics:

**Fair Price**

Displays the estimated fair price of the asset on the selected timeframe.

**Position**

Shows the asset's current position relative to the indicator's calculated ranges, helping you evaluate whether price is approaching overbought or oversold conditions.

**Main Trend**

Displays the prevailing market trend according to the indicator's calculations.

**Volatility**

Measures the asset's current volatility and trading range.

For example, on the Bitcoin Weekly chart, the displayed volatility may indicate a trading range of approximately $8,000–$9,000.

Higher volatility can create more potential trading opportunities, while very low volatility may indicate market uncertainty and make directional trading more difficult.

**Volume**

Shows whether current trading volume is above or below its historical average.

**Signal**

Displays when a confirmed Buy or Sell signal is generated.
