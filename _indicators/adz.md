## Crypto4light ADZ – Accumulation & Distribution Zones

Crypto4light ADZ is a technical analysis indicator designed to identify potential accumulation and distribution phases in an asset.

It divides the chart into several color-coded zones, each representing a different stage of market activity and potential positioning by market participants.

The purpose is to help traders understand where large market participants may be accumulating or distributing positions and where smaller participants may begin reacting to changing market conditions.

## Understanding the Zones

**Red Zone – Distribution**

The solid red zone represents a potential distribution area where large market participants may be completing the process of selling or reducing their positions.

Depending on the selected timeframe, this zone can help identify areas where selling pressure may increase and where a trend reversal or continuation of distribution could develop.

**Green Zone – Accumulation**

The solid green zone represents a potential accumulation area where large market participants may be building their positions.

It highlights price areas where buying interest may increase and where a new accumulation phase could develop.

**Blue Zone – Market Participants' Activity**

The middle blue zone represents an area where medium and smaller market participants may begin accumulating positions or taking profits and reducing exposure.

It helps visualize the transition between the broader accumulation and distribution areas.

**Yellow Zone – Potential Trend Transition**

The yellow zone, located within the blue area, highlights a potential change in market direction.

It may indicate that larger participants have already accumulated or distributed a significant portion of their positions and that the market could be preparing for the next directional move.

The interpretation depends on the timeframe being analyzed.

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Crypto4light ADZ can be used across different timeframes to evaluate accumulation and distribution behavior from both short-term and broader market perspectives.

Higher timeframes can help identify larger accumulation and distribution phases, while lower timeframes provide additional detail for analyzing price behavior within these zones.

## How to Use Crypto4light ADZ

Use the colored zones as reference areas when analyzing market structure, price action, and potential changes in trend direction.

Pay attention to how price behaves when entering or leaving each zone. Combining these observations with your preferred technical analysis tools and risk management approach can help you make more structured trading decisions.

## Important

Crypto4light ADZ is a market analysis and visualization tool. Its zones represent algorithmically identified potential accumulation and distribution areas, not verified positions or transactions of specific institutional investors.

The indicator does not guarantee reversals or future price movements. Always perform your own analysis and apply proper risk management.
