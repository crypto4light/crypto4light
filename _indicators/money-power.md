## Money Power – Volume Flow & Whale Activity Indicator

Money Power is a multi-timeframe indicator designed to analyze volume dynamics and identify potential accumulation and distribution phases in an asset.

By analyzing market data across different timeframes, the indicator provides a visual representation of potential volume inflows and outflows, helping traders monitor changes in market momentum and identify areas where buying or selling pressure may be increasing.

The higher the selected timeframe, the more useful the indicator can be for observing broader volume trends and potential changes in market direction.

## Overbought & Oversold Zones

By default, the indicator features two key zones:

* **Red Zone – Overbought:** Highlights areas where volume dynamics may indicate significant distribution or potential selling pressure.
* **Green Zone – Oversold:** Highlights areas where volume dynamics may indicate accumulation or potential buying opportunities.

When the indicator reaches one of these zones, it can help identify potential areas to consider taking partial profits, reducing exposure, or gradually building a position.

These zones should be evaluated in the context of the selected timeframe and overall market structure.

## Volume Flow & Price Divergences

One of the most useful ways to interpret Money Power is by comparing its curves with price action.

For example:

**Potential Distribution – Bearish Divergence**

If the asset continues making higher highs while the Money Power curves form lower highs in the overbought zone, this may indicate weakening volume inflows or increasing distribution pressure.

This can serve as a warning that buying momentum is fading and that larger market participants may be reducing their exposure.

**Potential Accumulation – Bullish Divergence**

The opposite situation can occur in the oversold zone.

If price continues making lower lows while the Money Power curves form higher lows, this may indicate improving volume dynamics despite continued weakness in price action.

Such divergence can help identify potential accumulation phases and areas worth monitoring for long opportunities.

These signals do not guarantee an immediate reversal. Price can continue moving in the same direction, so confirmation from price action and broader market structure remains important.

## RSI & MACD Divergence Signals

The indicator also includes additional RSI and MACD divergence detection to help identify potential changes in momentum.

These signals provide another layer of confirmation when analyzing possible market reversals, accumulation, and distribution.

## Whale Activity Signals

The latest version introduces additional visual signals designed to highlight potential changes in volume dynamics.

Default signal colors are described below, but all colors can be customized to match your TradingView chart theme.

* **Yellow Block** – Potential whale selling or partial position reduction.
* **Yellow Block with Down Arrow** – Strong whale selling signal.
* **Blue Block** – Potential whale buying activity.
* **Blue Block with Up Arrow** – Strong whale buying signal.
* **Down Triangle** – Bearish RSI Divergence.
* **Up Triangle** – Bullish RSI Divergence.
* **Red Circle** – Bearish MACD Divergence.
* **Green Circle** – Bullish MACD Divergence.

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Money Power can be used across different timeframes depending on your trading strategy.

Higher timeframes such as Weekly, 3D, and Daily can help identify broader volume trends and potential accumulation and distribution phases, while lower timeframes can provide additional insight into shorter-term market movements.

For swing traders and position traders, analyzing divergences and volume dynamics on higher timeframes can be particularly useful when evaluating potential entry and exit areas.

## How to Use Money Power

Money Power is designed to help traders:

* Monitor potential volume inflows and outflows.
* Identify possible accumulation and distribution phases.
* Recognize overbought and oversold conditions.
* Detect bullish and bearish RSI/MACD divergences.
* Monitor potential whale buying and selling activity.
* Evaluate potential entry, exit, and partial profit-taking areas across multiple timeframes.

**Important:** Money Power is a technical analysis tool that interprets market data to highlight potential volume dynamics. Its whale activity signals should not be treated as direct confirmation of individual wallet transactions or institutional positions.

It is not a standalone trading system and does not guarantee profitable results. Always combine its readings with price action, market structure, and proper risk management.

**It's not magic. It's a tool to work with.**
