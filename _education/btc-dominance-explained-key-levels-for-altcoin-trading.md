# BTC Dominance Explained: Key Levels for Altcoin Trading

Bitcoin dominance (BTC.D) is an important market metric that measures Bitcoin's share of the total cryptocurrency market capitalization. Understanding its movements can help traders evaluate capital rotation between Bitcoin and altcoins.

In this video, we analyze Bitcoin dominance from a technical perspective, focusing on important support and resistance levels, market structure, and potential scenarios that could influence altcoin performance.

We explore how changes in BTC dominance may provide additional context for understanding altcoin market trends and identifying periods when alternative cryptocurrencies could outperform Bitcoin.

**Topics covered:**

* What Bitcoin dominance (BTC.D) measures
* Important BTC dominance support and resistance levels
* BTC dominance technical analysis
* Bitcoin and altcoin market dynamics
* Potential capital rotation between BTC and altcoins
* How to use BTC.D when analyzing the crypto market

Bitcoin dominance is one of several market indicators and should not be used as a standalone signal for trading decisions.
