# How to Use the Volume Indicator: Spot Whales and Fakeouts FAST

Price alone does not tell the whole story. Trading volume provides additional information about market activity and helps traders evaluate the strength behind price movements.

Whether you're analyzing Bitcoin or altcoins, understanding volume can help you recognize potential breakouts, unusual trading activity, and situations where price movements lack confirmation.

## 1. What Does Trading Volume Tell You?

Volume measures how much of an asset has been traded during a specific period. Increasing volume can indicate stronger market participation, while declining volume may suggest reduced activity.

## 2. How to Identify Potential Whale Activity

Unusually large volume spikes can indicate significant trading activity. However, volume alone cannot confirm that whales are buying or selling. Combine it with price action, order flow, and other market data for better context.

## 3. How to Spot Fake Breakouts

A breakout above resistance accompanied by strong volume may indicate stronger participation. If price breaks a level on weak volume and quickly falls back into the previous range, it could be a warning sign of a failed breakout.

## 4. Combine Volume with Price Action

Volume becomes more useful when analyzed alongside support and resistance, candlestick patterns, and market structure. This combination can help you distinguish between stronger price movements and moves that lack confirmation.

## Final Thoughts

Volume is one of the most useful tools in technical analysis, but it should never be treated as a standalone signal.
