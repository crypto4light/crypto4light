# Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) in Crypto: Build a Portfolio Step by Step

Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) is an investment strategy that involves purchasing an asset at regular intervals, regardless of short-term price fluctuations.

In this video, we explore how to apply the DCA strategy to cryptocurrency investing and how to gradually build a diversified crypto portfolio. We explain the core principles behind recurring purchases, how DCA affects your average entry price, and why this approach can help reduce the impact of short-term market volatility.

Whether you are new to cryptocurrency or looking to improve your portfolio management approach, this step-by-step guide explains how to structure a DCA plan and apply it consistently.

**Topics covered:**

* What is Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)?
* How DCA works in cryptocurrency markets
* Building a crypto portfolio step by step
* Calculating your average purchase price
* Managing investments during market volatility
* Long-term cryptocurrency portfolio accumulation
* Advantages and limitations of the DCA strategy

DCA does not guarantee profits or eliminate investment risk. Cryptocurrency prices can fluctuate significantly, and portfolio performance depends on market conditions.
