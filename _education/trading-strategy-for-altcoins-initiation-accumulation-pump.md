Frequently, users receive an airdrop or retrodrop and promptly sell it once the asset becomes available on an exchange. Moreover, there are often instances where individuals miss out on a drop from a project that later experiences significant gains upon listing, leading to a fear of missing out (FOMO).This video is aimed at beginner traders and focuses on the Initiation-Accumulation-Pump (IAP) strategy. It provides insights into making wise investments in cryptocurrency projects during their initial listings on exchanges.
