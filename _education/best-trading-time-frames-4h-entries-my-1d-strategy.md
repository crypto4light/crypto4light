# Best Trading Timeframes: 4H Entries + My 1D Strategy

Choosing the right timeframe is an important part of technical analysis. Instead of analyzing every chart independently, traders can combine multiple timeframes to understand the bigger market picture and identify more precise entries.

This strategy focuses on two key timeframes: **1D (Daily) and 4H (4-Hour).**

## 1. Why Use the 1D Timeframe?

The daily chart helps identify the broader market structure and direction. It allows traders to analyze major support and resistance levels, trend changes, and important price zones without getting distracted by short-term volatility.

The 1D timeframe provides the foundation for your trading decisions.

## 2. Why Use the 4H Timeframe for Entries?

Once the broader trend is identified, the 4H chart can help you analyze shorter-term price movements and potential entry opportunities.

You can use it to monitor:

* Local support and resistance.
* Breakouts and retests.
* Changes in market structure.
* Volume confirmation.
* Potential trend reversals.

## 3. How to Combine 1D and 4H

A simple multi-timeframe workflow:

1. **Analyze the 1D chart:** Identify the overall trend and important levels.
2. **Mark key zones:** Determine where price could react.
3. **Switch to 4H:** Wait for a potential setup near those zones.
4. **Confirm the entry:** Look for supporting price action and define your risk.

This approach helps separate the bigger market direction from short-term entry decisions.

## Final Thoughts

You don't need to monitor ten different timeframes to trade effectively. A structured combination of the daily and 4-hour charts can provide a clear framework for market analysis.
