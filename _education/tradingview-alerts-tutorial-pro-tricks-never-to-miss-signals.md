# TradingView Alerts Tutorial: Pro Tricks to Never Miss Trading Signals

Monitoring charts all day is not an efficient trading strategy. TradingView alerts allow you to track important market levels and receive notifications when specific conditions are met.

Whether you trade Bitcoin, altcoins, or traditional financial markets, properly configured alerts can help you stay focused without constantly watching price movements.

## 1. Create Alerts for Important Price Levels

Set alerts around key support and resistance zones, breakout levels, and important price targets. This helps you react when the market reaches areas that matter to your trading strategy.

## 2. Use Indicator-Based Alerts

TradingView supports alerts based on many technical indicators and custom scripts. You can monitor conditions such as RSI levels, moving average crossovers, and other indicator signals.

## 3. Choose the Right Notification Method

TradingView can deliver notifications through different channels, depending on your subscription and settings. Configure the available options so important alerts reach you when you need them.

## 4. Avoid Alert Overload

Creating too many alerts can become counterproductive. Focus on meaningful levels and conditions instead of receiving notifications for every minor price movement.

## Final Thoughts

TradingView alerts can make your market analysis more organized and efficient. The key is knowing which conditions deserve your attention.
