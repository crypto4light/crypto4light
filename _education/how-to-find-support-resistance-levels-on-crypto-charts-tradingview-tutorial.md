Support and resistance are among the most important concepts in cryptocurrency technical analysis. Understanding how to identify and draw these levels can help traders interpret price action, recognize important market zones and build a more structured trading strategy.

In this tutorial, we explore how to draw support and resistance on crypto charts using TradingView. Learn how to identify significant price levels, recognize areas where buyers and sellers have previously reacted, distinguish key zones from minor price movements and use historical price action to analyze potential market reactions.

**Topics covered:**

* What support and resistance levels represent
* How to identify important price zones
* Drawing horizontal support and resistance levels in TradingView
* Understanding price reactions and historical levels
* Identifying stronger and weaker market zones
* Common mistakes when drawing support and resistance

Whether you're analyzing Bitcoin, Ethereum or altcoins, learning to identify key price levels is an essential part of developing your technical analysis skills.
