The altcoin market continues to face significant pressure, and the broader market structure suggests that the correction may not be over yet. While some cryptocurrencies are attempting to stabilize, many altcoins remain vulnerable to another wave of selling pressure.

In this analysis, we focus on two established altcoins: **Algorand (ALGO) and Hedera (HBAR)**. Both have experienced substantial price declines, and their long-term technical structures are approaching important support areas that could play a decisive role in determining their next moves.\
\
What Happens If These Support Levels Fail?

The broader altcoin market remains highly sensitive to Bitcoin's direction, liquidity conditions, and overall investor risk appetite.

If Bitcoin experiences another significant correction, smaller cryptocurrencies could face additional selling pressure.

For ALGO and HBAR, the key scenarios to monitor are:

* **Bullish scenario:** Buyers defend critical support, selling pressure decreases, and price begins forming a higher-timeframe recovery structure.
* **Bearish scenario:** Support breaks, previous demand zones fail to attract buyers, and the market continues searching for lower price levels.
* **Neutral scenario:** Both assets remain range-bound while the market waits for stronger directional confirmation.
